rclone (personal use)

This OAuth app is used only by its owner to access the owner's own Google Drive with rclone. It is not offered to anyone else.

Privacy Policy

This app does not collect, store, or share any user data with third parties. Data accessed from Google Drive stays on the owner's own computer.

このアプリは所有者本人が自分の Google ドライブを rclone で読むためだけに使うもので、他人には提供しません。データの収集・第三者提供は行いません。
